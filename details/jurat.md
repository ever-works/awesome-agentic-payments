## Overview

jurat provides evidence infrastructure for AI agents.

## Features

- Notarized snapshots of any public URL (ed25519-signed attestations, archived bytes, daily merkle roots, verification free forever)
- Sports finals with proof attached across 24 leagues incl. rugby and cricket
- Change watchers and scheduled wakeups for ephemeral agents
- Pre-flight: 18 conformance checks on any endpoint before payment
- Conformance sweeps a whole service returning a graded, ed25519-signed report
- Invalid input rejected with free 400 (buyer never pays for malformed request)

## Pricing

- 10 paid endpoints at $0.005–$0.50 USDC on Base via CDP facilitator
- 14 free forever
- No API keys, no signup
