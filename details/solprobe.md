## Overview

- Rug check / A–F safety grade ($0.02)
- Market signal ($0.20)
- Full due-diligence with wash-trading and bundled-launch detection ($0.50)
- Bring-your-own-wallet Jupiter swap executor ($0.15)
- Gas sponsored by the CDP facilitator — no SOL or ETH needed
- x402 v2 with Bazaar discovery extension
- No keys or signup
- Same price on Base or Solana mainnet
