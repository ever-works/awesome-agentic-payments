## Overview

Hookproof is a Stripe webhook reliability source kit delivered after a 5 USDC x402 v2 payment on Base, including templates, adapters, fixtures, and deployment documentation.

## Features

- Next.js route templates
- Typed dispatch
- Postgres idempotency adapter
- Upstash idempotency adapter
- Retry fixtures
- Deployment runbook
- x402 v2 payment on Base
