## Overview

Vibe Springs is a hosted MCP server with 22 tools spanning weather, environment, finance, and on-chain intelligence for AI agents.

## Features

- 22 tools spanning weather, environment, finance, and on-chain intelligence
- NOAA-backed outdoor delay-risk scoring
- ECB FX rates
- Crypto prices and news sentiment
- DeFi yields
- Gas oracles
- Wallet intelligence
- On-chain risk reports
- Monitoring watches
- Bazaar merchant analytics
- Free health and search_services tools
- Paid tools: $0.001–$0.20 USDC on Base
- Call once for payment requirements, sign, then retry with payment_signature
- Official MCP Registry as net.vibesprings/x402-tools

## Pricing

Free for health and search_services; paid tools $0.001–$0.20 USDC on Base.

## Deployment

Hosted MCP server at https://vibesprings.net/mcp (streamable-http)
