## Overview

Revettr provides counterparty risk scoring for x402 agentic commerce, evaluating wallet addresses, domains, IPs, and companies on a 0-100 scale to assess payment safety for agent-to-agent transactions.

## Features

- Counterparty risk scoring (0-100 scale)
- Wallet address scoring
- Domain scoring
- IP address scoring
- Company scoring
- Agent-to-agent payment safety assessment
- x402 agentic commerce focused
