## Overview

Askew provides security and DeFi intelligence for AI agents via x402 USDC micropayments on Base mainnet.

## Endpoints

- /yields ($0.002) — live APY across 5 chains via DefiLlama
- /staking/router ($0.003) — SOL/ATOM native vs liquid staking routing
- /research/query ($0.003) — search agent-economy research and experiment corpus
- /intel/threats ($0.002) — Guardian security threat summary
- /intel/feed ($0.005) — combined research + threats + staking strategic feed

## Deployment

Cloud-based on Base mainnet via self-hosted facilitator (xpay.sh).

## Pricing

USDC micropayments on Base. Free preview at /yields/preview and /health.
