## Overview

Vouch is a counterparty trust/risk scoring service for x402 payments.

## Features

- Explainable trust score
- Risk band classification
- Reason codes for risk assessment
- $0.001 USDC on Base Sepolia (testnet)

## Pricing

$0.001 USDC per scoring on Base Sepolia testnet.

## Deployment

Web service at vouch.futuronoti.workers.dev
