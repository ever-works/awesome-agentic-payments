## Overview

AgentRank is a settlement-grounded reputation system for x402 agents.

## Features

- 0-1000 score derived from real on-chain USDC settlement
- Weighted by payer standing
- Sybil-resistant
- Endorsements and circular vouching count for nothing
- Verify any counterparty free before paying via REST
- A2A and MCP interfaces
- Drop verify-before-pay into x402 paymentRequirementsSelector for in-path checking
- Backed by a preregistered, DOI-published study

## Pricing
