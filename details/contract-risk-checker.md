## Overview

Contract Risk Checker is an AI-powered legal contract analysis service designed for autonomous agents. It analyzes contracts for risk, detects problematic clauses, and generates actionable fix suggestions.

## Features

- Killer clause detection
- Risk score calculation (0–100, RED/YELLOW/GREEN)
- Negotiation script generation
- Exact fix suggestions
- Supports PDF, DOCX, and TXT formats
- Handles documents up to ~600 pages
- No subscription required
- No account required
- x402 USDC payment on Base and Solana

## Pricing

- $10 USDC per analysis

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (Google Cloud Run)
