## Overview

bill-negotiation-api is a voice-agent service that dials ISP, cell, and insurance retention lines to negotiate lower bills on behalf of users.

## Features

- POST /negotiate endpoint
- Voice-agent dials retention lines
- Returns call transcript
- Returns booked saving amount
- ISP / cell / insurance support
- Flat fee per attempt
- No subscription

## Pricing

Flat $20 USDC per attempt on Base.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted on Cloudflare Workers.
