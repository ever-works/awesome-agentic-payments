## Overview

agenteconomy.to is a real-time analytics dashboard for the agentic economy, tracking activity across x402, ERC-8004, ERC-8183, and MPP protocols on 8 chains.

## Features

- Aggregated event counter across multiple protocols
- Chain distribution analytics
- Facilitator share comparison
- Time-series charts
- Data refreshes every 6 hours
