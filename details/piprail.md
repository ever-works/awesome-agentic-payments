# PipRail

## Overview
Backendless, no-fee x402 SDK for AI agents.

## Features
- 28 chains in 10 families (every major EVM chain plus Solana, TON, Tron, NEAR, Sui, Aptos, Algorand, Stellar & XRPL)
- Self-custodial — payer broadcasts their own transfer
- Merchant verifies locally against their own RPC, no facilitator
- `requirePayment()` to take payments
- `PipRailClient` to make payments
- Per-call and lifetime spend budgets
- `planPayment()` affordability + recipient-readiness preflight

## Pricing
Free (no fees).

## Deployment
Client-side SDK, no server deployment required.

- npm package: `@piprail/sdk`
