## Overview

GEDX402 is a Cloudflare Workers AI hub with x402 v2 USDC micropayments across multiple chains.

## Features

- Cloudflare Workers AI hub
- x402 v2 USDC micropayments
- Multi-chain: Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, World, Solana
- Eight modality shards

## Pricing

Not explicitly stated; uses x402 v2 USDC.
