## Overview

Micro Data API Factory — JP Data Enrich provides Japan public-company and supplier intelligence for AI agents, resolving companies by name, domain, or corporate number.

## Pricing

- $0.01–$0.05 USDC per call on Base USDC

## Features

- Company resolution by name, domain, or corporate number
- gBizINFO profiles
- Business signals
- EDINET filings
- EDINET officer rows
- Batch enrichment
- Wallet-free preview routes under /preview/v1/enrich/...
- x402-paid production routes under /v1/enrich/...

## Integrations

- USDC on Base for micropayments
- x402 payment enforcement
- gBizINFO, EDINET public filings, official public-data feeds
