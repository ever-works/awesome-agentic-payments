## Overview

Nobulex is an open-source cryptographic receipt layer for AI agent actions.

## Features

- Ed25519-signed receipts
- RFC 8785 canonicalized
- Content-addressed action_ref
- Hash-chained audit trails
- Third independent issuer in x402 section 5 (14/14 conformance verdicts)
- Normative implementation guidance in OWASP Agentic Skills Top 10 (AST09)
- Verifiable offline without operator trust
