## Overview

Melvea provides citation-backed honey intelligence with structured varietal data and verifiable research provenance for 150+ honey varieties.

## Features

- Structured varietal data for 150+ honey varieties
- Verifiable research provenance (DOI/PMID)
- Variety catalog endpoint
- Per-variety research citations
- Grounded flavor/use recommendations
- Every compositional datum resolvable to a real published source
- $0.02–$0.10 USDC per call on Base
