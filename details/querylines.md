## Overview

QueryLines provides surplus lines market data for AI agents via USDC on Base via CDP facilitator with Bazaar discovery.

## Data

- U.S. excess & surplus (E&S) insurance premium and policy-count statistics from state stamping office publications
- Time series
- YoY summaries
- Cross-state comparison by state, coverage line, and month/quarter/year

## Deployment

Cloud-based on Base mainnet via CDP facilitator.

## Pricing

$0.10 single-state query, $0.50 cross-state compare USDC on Base. Free discovery endpoints. No API keys or signup required.
