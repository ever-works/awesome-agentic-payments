## Overview

PureSignal402 provides high-confidence prediction-market signals from Kalshi across multiple categories including crypto, macro, politics, weather, sports, and tech.

## Pricing

- $0.005 USDC per call on Base mainnet via Coinbase CDP facilitator

## Features

- Markets above configurable confidence threshold
- TTL, 24h volume, and liquidity for tradability
- /v1/live surfaces markets settling soon (?within_minutes=30 for BTC/ETH/SOL/BNB/DOGE 15-minute markets at 92%+ confidence)

## Integrations

- USDC on Base for micropayments
- x402 payment enforcement
- Coinbase CDP facilitator
- OpenAPI spec
- Agent Card
- MCP
