## Overview

OmniNexu provides structured, traceable S&P 500 financial intelligence for AI agents.

## Features

- 8 x402-paywalled endpoints
- Company fundamentals
- SEC Form 4 insider trades
- 13F institutional holdings
- Industry peer ranking
- Multi-year CAGR trends
- Smart-money bundles
- SEC EDGAR-sourced with cross-source confidence scoring
- Open source (Apache 2.0)

## Pricing

- $0.01–$0.05 USDC on Base
- No API keys, no signup
