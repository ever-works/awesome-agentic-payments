## Overview

Know Your Human (Convrgent) is a personality intelligence API for AI agents with 36 endpoints across 11 personality frameworks including Socionics, Enneagram, Human Design, Vedic, BaZi, and more. It uses deterministic heuristic engines with no LLM dependency.

## Features

- 36 endpoints across 11 personality frameworks
- Socionics, Enneagram, Human Design, Vedic, BaZi, and more
- Deterministic heuristic engines
- No LLM dependency
- x402 payment integration
- A2A and x402 Discovery available
