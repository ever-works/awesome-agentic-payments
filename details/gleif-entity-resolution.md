## Overview

GLEIF Entity Resolution resolves company names to their canonical Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) for AI agents.

## Features

- Resolve company name to canonical LEI
- Legal name, status, jurisdiction, address, registration status
- Corporate hierarchy (direct/ultimate parent + children)
- Fuzzy-match confidence score and disambiguation candidates
