## Overview

AIScan is an AI visibility auditing platform for websites, offering 18 pay-per-use x402 capabilities on Base. It measures AI answer visibility, agent readiness, and provides fix packs and llms.txt/mcp.json generation.

## Features

- AI visibility auditing for websites
- 18 pay-per-use x402 capabilities on Base
- Pricing: 0.06-3.50 USDC
- 4 scores: AEO, GEO, Agent Readiness, MCP Readiness (only scanner that checks MCP)
- Brand visibility measurement across 30 AI answers
- Mention rate, share of voice metrics
- Fix packs for optimization
- llms.txt and mcp.json generation
- A2A agent card
- Free discovery at /api/agent/index
- No API keys, no signup
- OpenAPI specification available

## Pricing
