## Overview

Hermes Japanese Research delivers AI summaries of Nikkei news, corporate earnings, and BOJ policy for the Japanese market.

## Features

- Nikkei news monitoring
- Corporate earnings analysis
- BOJ policy summaries
- AI-generated summaries
- x402 pay-per-call pricing

## Pricing

$0.02 USDC per call via x402.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted API (Cloudflare Workers).
