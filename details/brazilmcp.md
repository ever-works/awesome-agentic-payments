## Overview

BrazilMCP provides official-source Brazilian company data (KYB) for AI agents.

## Features

- Receita Federal registry lookup by CNPJ or company name ($0.005)
- Sanctions screening across all 5 Brazilian federal lists ($0.02)
- Full due-diligence dossier with government procurement history and compliance risk flags ($0.25)
- Own dataset rebuilt monthly from the full official publication (68M+ companies), not a wrapper
- x402 v1+v2 USDC on Base via CDP facilitator
- No API keys, no signup
- Paid MCP server, listed in the official MCP Registry

## Pricing

- $0.005–$0.25 USDC per call depending on endpoint
