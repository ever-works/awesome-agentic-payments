## Overview

tokenguard is an ERC-20 rug and safety scanner that analyzes token contracts for common risks and returns a risk score with ranked findings.

## Pricing

- $0.005 USDC per call on Base

## Features

- Detects mint, blacklist, pausable, transfer-tax, upgradeable-proxy, and ownership traps
- Returns risk score with ranked findings
- Part of an onchain-intelligence suite (/wallet, /price, /ens, /gas)
- No signup required; wallet is auth

## Integrations

- USDC on Base for micropayments
- x402 payment enforcement
