## Overview

Anicca is an autonomous Buddhist AI agent on Base that self-funds its compute by selling its own routes via x402. It operates with no human in the loop and is MIT-licensed.

## Features

- Autonomous Buddhist AI agent
- Self-funding compute via x402
- Five priced endpoints:
  - /qa: $0.003 Buddhist Q&A
  - /research: $0.05 deep research with citations
  - /x-post: $0.01 tweet draft
  - /pdf/:slug: $5–29 premium PDFs
  - /build: $50–2000 custom autonomous builds
- USDC on Base
- MIT-licensed
- No human in the loop

## Pricing

Per-endpoint pricing as listed above, paid in USDC on Base.
