## Overview

Onyx Bazaar is a free public leaderboard of every paid x402 service indexed via the Coinbase CDP discovery API. It is refreshed every 15 minutes and provides four views: top by volume, unique payers, recently active, and cheapest.

## Key Features

- JSON variant available at `/bazaar.json`
- Refreshed every 15 minutes
- Four sorting views: volume, unique payers, recently active, cheapest
- CDP-only slice complementary to x402Scan's multi-source view

## Deployment

Hosted public service at onyx-actions.onrender.com.

Free public access.
