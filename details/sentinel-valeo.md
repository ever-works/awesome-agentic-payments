## Overview

Sentinel is an enterprise audit & compliance layer for x402 payments built by Valeo.

## Features

- Budget enforcement (per-call, hourly, daily)
- Structured audit trails
- Real-time dashboard
- Public payment explorer
- SDK: @x402sentinel/x402

## Pricing

Paid enterprise solution.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted at sentinel.valeocash.com
