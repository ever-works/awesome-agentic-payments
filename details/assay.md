## Overview

Assay is a service-quality oracle for x402. It pays real USDC to probe machine-payable services on a schedule and scores what actually comes back across payment settlement, schema conformance, ground-truth accuracy, and LLM-judged quality.

## Features

- Composite quality score (0-100)
- Quality tiers: gold/ok/avoid
- On-chain receipts for every rating
- Daily evidence digests Bitcoin-anchored via OpenTimestamps
- Free tier verdicts at `/tier/{url}`
- Paid full reports at $0.005 USDC on Base
- Listed in the CDP Bazaar

## Pricing

Free tier available; full reports cost $0.005 USDC on Base.
