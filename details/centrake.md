## Overview

CentRake is an AI-powered universal calculator service that accepts x402 payments from AI agents. It features a 3-layer self-correcting verification engine and 5-tier dynamic pricing model.

## Features

- 3-layer self-correcting verification engine
- 5-tier dynamic pricing: $0.01 basic solve, $0.05 verified solve (calculus/finance), $0.10 research solve (proofs/theorems), $0.15 AI action plans, $0.02 AI search
- 438+ problem categories across math, finance, science, statistics, health, and everyday domains
- Free for humans, paid for AI agents
- Settlement in USDC on Base
- x402-compatible with /.well-known/x402 discovery, /api/x402/tiers pricing, and /api/x402/info endpoints

## Pricing

- $0.01 per basic solve
- $0.05 per verified solve (calculus/finance)
- $0.10 per research solve (proofs/theorems)
- $0.15 per AI action plan
- $0.02 per AI search
- Free for human users

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted service; no self-hosting option documented.

## Integrations

- x402 payment protocol
