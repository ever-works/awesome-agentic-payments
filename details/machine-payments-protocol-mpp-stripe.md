## Overview

Machine Payments Protocol (MPP) is a payment-method agnostic payment rail sponsored by Tempo and Stripe. It sits beneath commerce protocols (ACP, UCP, AP2) and supports any rail.

## Features

- Payment-method agnostic
- Supports cards, stablecoin, fiat acceptance via standard PSP plumbing
- Settlement: varies by method
- Best fit: cards, stablecoin, fiat acceptance

## Specification

- Spec: https://mpp.dev
- Sponsor: Tempo + Stripe

## Pricing

Varies by payment method and Stripe pricing.
