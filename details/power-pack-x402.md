## Overview

Power Pack x402 is an outreach email scoring API that evaluates email quality across multiple dimensions and provides improvement suggestions.

## Features

- Subject quality scoring
- Body quality scoring
- Predicted open rate
- Spam risk assessment
- Tone analysis
- Length analysis
- Personalization scoring
- CTA clarity scoring
- Top 3 improvement suggestions
- Stronger rewritten subject
- Powered by Claude Haiku 4.5

## Pricing

$0.01 USDC per call on Base mainnet.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted API. Source: https://github.com/bshelby88/power-pack-x402
