## Overview

TradingAgents x402 is a multi-agent LLM ticker consensus system that coordinates five specialist analysts plus debate and synthesis stages to produce BUY/HOLD/SELL recommendations.

## Features

- Five specialist analysts: fundamentals, sentiment, news, technical
- Bullish-vs-bearish researcher debate
- Trader synthesis
- Risk-management review
- Portfolio-manager final decision
- Structured BUY/HOLD/SELL output with confidence
- Full per-agent reports
- Powered by TauricResearch/TradingAgents (arXiv:2412.20138)
