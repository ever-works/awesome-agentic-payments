## Overview

Hermes Asia Intelligence provides business intelligence for China/Taiwan/Japan markets, covering trade policy, market dynamics, and regulatory changes with Chinese/Japanese bilingual output.

## Features

- Trade policy monitoring for China/Taiwan/Japan
- Market dynamics analysis
- Regulatory change tracking
- Bilingual Chinese/Japanese output
- x402 pay-per-call pricing

## Pricing

$0.02 USDC per call via x402.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted API (Cloudflare Workers).
