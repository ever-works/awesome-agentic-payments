## Overview

Cinderwright Discovery Hub is a cross-protocol discovery hub that indexes over 2,771 AI agent payment services across x402, MPP, and L402/Lightning protocols.

## Features

- Free keyword search (`/discover?q=weather`)
- Quality grades A-F on all indexed services
- Intent-based search
- Price intelligence
- Payment proxy: deposit USDC once, call any x402 service with one header — no signing, no gas management
- MCP server installable in Claude Desktop

## Pricing

Free for keyword search; payment proxy requires USDC deposit.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted at api.ideafactorylab.org
