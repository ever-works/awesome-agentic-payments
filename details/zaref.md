## Overview

zaref provides South African reference data for AI agents.

## Features

- Fuel prices, interest rates, tax tables, FX rates, bond yields, deeds fees, toll fees, municipal tariffs, wages and grants
- 33 verified series with per-series history
- 29 calculators
- 2 live feeds (government tenders and crypto ZAR prices)
- 5 task-shaped POST answers (payroll, income tax, VAT, minimum-wage compliance, working days)
- Multi-series batch call and change-alert webhooks
- Every value carries official source URL, effective date, and staleness flag
- Nulls returned with a note rather than a guess
- Invalid input rejected with free 400

## Pricing

- 104 paid operations at $0.005–$0.10 USDC on Base via Coinbase CDP facilitator
- /catalog and /v1/public-holidays free
- No accounts, no API keys
