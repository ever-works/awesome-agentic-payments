## Overview

NicheData KDP Intelligence provides niche demand and competition intelligence for Kindle Direct Publishing authors via x402 USDC micropayments on Base.

## Data

- Demand scores
- Competition intensity
- BSR ranges
- Revenue estimates
- Pricing analytics

## Deployment

Cloud-based on Base mainnet via CDP Facilitator.

## Pricing

$0.03 USDC per query. Free discovery endpoint lists all available niches.
