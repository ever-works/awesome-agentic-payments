## Overview

openFDA Drug Safety provides pay-per-call FDA drug safety profiles for AI agents.

## Features

- Label highlights (boxed warning, contraindications, interactions)
- Pregnancy/pediatric/geriatric flags
- Top adverse-event signals
- Recent recalls
- Shortage & marketing/discontinued status
- RxNorm-normalized drug-name resolution with match confidence score

## Pricing

- $0.04 USDC on Base via Coinbase CDP facilitator
- No API key, no account
- Data: openFDA (CC0)
