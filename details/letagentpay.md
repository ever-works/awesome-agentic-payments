## Overview

LetAgentPay is a rail-agnostic spend-control policy layer for AI agents that governs spending across multiple payment rails.

## Features

- Same policy engine governs x402, Stripe, and prepaid wallets
- Daily/weekly/monthly caps
- Category-based controls
- Schedule-based controls
- Human-in-the-loop approval
- Non-custodial x402 facilitator-ready
- USDC ↔ USD reconciliation
- Transaction hash deduplication
- Stablecoin de-peg protection
