## Overview

Kerdos Market Intelligence is an AI market intelligence API for agents and traders, accessible via x402 micropayments on Base.

## Features

- Live crypto sentiment scoring
- BTC/ETH regime direction
- Hyperliquid funding rates
- Gold/oil trading signals
- Whale alerts
- Liquidation cascade risk
- 8 total endpoints

## Pricing

$0.01-$0.05 USDC per call on Base.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted, pay-per-call via x402.
