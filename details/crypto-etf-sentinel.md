## Overview

Crypto ETF Sentinel is a US spot crypto ETF regulatory intelligence oracle that curates SEC filings and tracks approval pipelines across issuers and cryptocurrencies.

## Pricing

- 22 routes priced $0.05–$1.00 USDC on Base mainnet via CDP facilitator

## Features

- 3,100+ SEC filings across 105 issuers and 34 cryptocurrencies
- Approval-pipeline tracking
- XBRL financials
- POS AM operational data (sponsor fees, custodian, AP rosters)
- Fee-war monitoring

## Integrations

- USDC on Base for micropayments
- x402 payment enforcement
- Coinbase CDP facilitator
