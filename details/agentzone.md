## Overview

AgentZone is a unified explorer for trustless AI agents. It combines ERC-8004 identity, x402 payment history, reputation signals, and live service status across Base and Arbitrum networks.

## Key Features

- ERC-8004 identity integration for autonomous agents
- x402 payment history tracking and reputation signals
- Live service status monitoring across Base and Arbitrum
- Enables sellers to verify agent payment history before serving requests

## Deployment

Web-based explorer, publicly accessible.
