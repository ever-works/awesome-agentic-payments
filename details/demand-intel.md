## Overview

Demand Intel provides demand-side analytics and cross-rail supply intelligence for the x402 ecosystem.

## Features

- Cross-source demand signal aggregation across 32+ sources
- Weighted by family-count agreement
- Filtered by query-intent classification
- Builder Intel deduplication across CDP Bazaar, Agentic Market, and MPP via `provider_key`
- `unified_supply` SQL view spanning facilitators
- Pulse dashboard with KPI rail
- Signal Pipeline visualizer
- AI Executive Summary
